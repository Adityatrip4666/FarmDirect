# FarmDirect — Project Scope

## Project Overview

FarmDirect is a web-based agricultural marketplace that connects
farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) directly with
consumers and bulk buyers.

The platform aims to provide a digital marketplace where agricultural
producers can showcase their products and buyers can discover and
purchase agricultural products directly from producers.

## Project Objectives

The primary objectives of FarmDirect are:

1. Connect farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) directly
   with consumers and bulk buyers.

2. Provide farmers and FPOs with a digital platform to list and
   showcase their agricultural products.

3. Enable consumers and bulk buyers to discover agricultural products
   directly from producers.

4. Reduce dependency on unnecessary intermediaries in the agricultural
   supply chain.

5. Provide a simple and accessible web-based marketplace for
   agricultural product discovery and transactions.

6. Establish a foundation that can be extended with additional
   marketplace functionality in future versions.

   ## Target Users

FarmDirect is intended for the following user groups:

### 1. Farmers

Individual farmers who want to list and sell their agricultural
products directly through the platform.

### 2. Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)

FPOs that represent groups of farmers and want to showcase and sell
agricultural products to potential buyers.

### 3. Consumers

Individual customers looking to discover and purchase agricultural
products directly from farmers and FPOs.

### 4. Bulk Buyers

Businesses, retailers, restaurants, institutions, wholesalers, or
other organizations looking to source agricultural products in larger
quantities.

## Core Features

The core features of the FarmDirect platform are:

- User registration and authentication
- Farmer and FPO profiles
- Product listing and management
- Product discovery and browsing
- Product search and filtering
- Product details
- Buyer interaction with product listings
- Basic order or purchase workflow
- Basic marketplace management

## In-Scope Features

The following functionality is included in the scope of the first
version of FarmDirect:

### User Management

- User registration
- User login
- Basic user profiles
- Role-based identification of farmers, FPOs, consumers, and bulk
  buyers

### Farmer and FPO Functionality

- Create and manage producer profiles
- Add agricultural products
- Edit product information
- Remove product listings
- Specify product details such as name, category, quantity, and price

### Buyer Functionality

- Browse available products
- Search for products
- Filter products
- View product details
- Identify the farmer or FPO offering the product

### Marketplace Functionality

- Display available agricultural products
- Display product and producer information
- Support the basic purchase/order workflow

### Documentation

- Project scope documentation
- Basic project setup documentation

## Out-of-Scope Features

The following features are outside the scope of the first version:

- Native Android application
- Native iOS application
- Advanced AI-based recommendations
- AI-powered crop or disease detection
- Real-time logistics and fleet tracking
- Advanced supply-chain management
- International marketplace support
- Multi-country operations
- Advanced analytics and business intelligence
- Complex auction mechanisms
- Cryptocurrency or blockchain-based payments
- Advanced loyalty and rewards systems
- Voice-based marketplace interaction

## Project Limitations

The first version of FarmDirect will have the following limitations:

- The platform will initially be available as a web application.
- Advanced mobile functionality will not be provided through native
  applications.
- Logistics and delivery management will have limited functionality.
- Payment functionality may be limited depending on the selected
  payment integration.
- Product availability and inventory information will depend on
  information provided by farmers and FPOs.
- Advanced analytics and reporting will not be available in the first
  version.
- The platform may initially support a limited number of user roles
  and workflows.

  ## Scope Boundaries

FarmDirect Version 1 focuses on establishing the core marketplace
connection between agricultural producers and buyers.

The primary focus is:

Producer
   ↓
Product Listing
   ↓
Marketplace
   ↓
Consumer / Bulk Buyer

The first version focuses on product discovery, producer information,
and the basic marketplace workflow.

Advanced logistics, sophisticated analytics, native mobile
applications, AI functionality, and other advanced capabilities are
reserved for future versions.
